Pistachio Puree

Selected from the finest Antep pistachio orchards in Gaziantep, our premium pistachios are carefully processed using specialized grinding technology to create a smooth, flowing puree. Designed specifically for professional use in patisserie and ice cream industries, our product delivers exceptional results in any dessert or beverage recipe thanks to its soft, fluid, and homogeneous structure.

Our pistachio puree is particularly favored in ice cream production. Its smooth texture integrates seamlessly into ice cream mixtures, creating a unique flavor profile. Ideal for pastry chefs, this product can be used in various baked goods such as cakes, pastries, tarts, and profiteroles. It also provides an intense pistachio aroma in coffee beverages, smoothies, and milkshakes.

During production, the natural oil content of pistachios is preserved, and no chemical processing is applied. This ensures our product offers superior quality both in nutritional value and flavor. Rich in omega-3 fatty acids, vitamin E, and antioxidants, our puree is suitable for healthy eating programs.

Our quality control processes include detailed analyses for each product batch. Parameters such as viscosity, particle size, and flavor profile are continuously monitored. This meticulous approach guarantees consistent product quality every time and helps us earn our customers' trust.

Due to its concentrated structure, our product offers economical usage. Even small amounts provide an intense pistachio flavor. This feature creates cost advantages, especially for large-scale production facilities. At the same time, it creates a balanced flavor profile without overpowering other ingredients in recipes.

Our product, stored under cold chain protection, should be brought to room temperature before use. This process increases the puree's fluidity and facilitates the mixing process. After opening, it should be stored in the refrigerator and consumed quickly. Proper storage conditions ensure the product stays fresh and maintains its flavor.

We offer flexible packaging solutions for different needs. We serve customers of all scales, from small patisseries to large food manufacturers. Special pricing and fast delivery options are available for custom orders and bulk purchases. Customer satisfaction is the cornerstone of our business processes.
