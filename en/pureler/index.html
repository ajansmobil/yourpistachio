Broken Pistachio

Premium quality Antep pistachio broken pistachio, specially prepared for patisseries, chocolate manufacturers and food industry. Broken pistachio, which offers a unique experience to your products with its natural flavor and crispy texture, is widely used in professional kitchens and industrial production.

Our broken pistachio is produced by carefully selecting from the highest quality pistachios of Gaziantep. In our production process, quality control is carried out at every stage, ensuring consistent size and flavor guarantee. Our broken pistachio, which is a completely natural product without additives, is suitable for your healthy eating preferences.

Broken pistachio, which can be used on or in cakes, pastries, cookies and other bakery products in patisseries, is also an ideal option for chocolate manufacturers. Broken pistachio used in chocolate, praline and other confectionery products will add visual appeal and flavor to your products. In addition, it can also be used in ice cream, yogurt and other dairy products.

Our product is rich in protein, fiber, vitamin E, potassium and healthy fats. These nutritional values are preserved in our production process, allowing us to offer you the healthiest broken pistachio. Our quality control standards guarantee the same high quality in every batch.

Our broken pistachio is packaged under hygienic conditions and delivered to you in airtight packaging. Our product, which maintains its freshness and crispness for a long time under appropriate storage conditions, is offered with different sizes and packaging options according to your business needs. Special pricing and fast delivery options are available for bulk orders.

As Your Pistachio, we prioritize customer satisfaction. You can contact us to get detailed information about our broken pistachio, request a sample or place an order. Our professional team is ready to offer you the most suitable solution.
