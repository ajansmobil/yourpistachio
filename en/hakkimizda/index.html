About Us

As Your Pistachio, we set out to offer you the highest quality Antep pistachios grown in the rich lands of Gaziantep. We produce natural and delicious pistachio products by combining years of experience and traditional production methods with modern technology. Gaziantep is one of Turkey's most important pistachio production centers, and the pistachios grown in this region stand out with their world-renowned quality.

Our product range includes puree, broken and whole pistachio varieties. Each of our products is produced from carefully selected pistachios and prepared in accordance with the highest quality standards. Customer satisfaction is our top priority. In our production process, we adopt an approach born from the combination of traditional methods and modern technology.

We offer pistachio products prepared with organic production methods, without additives, for consumers who value natural and healthy nutrition. We are at your service with premium quality pistachio varieties that you can use in your kitchen, patisseries and businesses. Our products are rich not only in terms of taste but also in nutritional value.

Pistachios are rich in protein, fiber, vitamins and minerals. Our products aim to contribute to a healthy lifestyle by preserving these valuable nutrients. Our quality control processes are carried out meticulously at every stage, ensuring that our products meet the highest standards.

The strong relationships we establish with our customers form the foundation of our business. We aim to develop long-term partnerships by offering customized solutions for each customer's needs. Our sustainable production approach, combined with an environmentally respectful attitude, works to leave a better world for future generations.

Gaziantep's rich cultural heritage and traditional production knowledge is reflected in the quality of our products. Thanks to our partnerships with local producers, we both contribute to the regional economy and deliver the freshest and highest quality pistachios to our customers. This approach of ours is not only a commercial success, but also part of our social responsibility.
